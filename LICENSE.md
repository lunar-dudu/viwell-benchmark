# Licence

ViWELL is released under a split licence, because the two parts of it are reused
differently: another research group should be able to adapt the scenario design for their own
language with attribution, while the tooling should carry as little friction as possible.

**Benchmark content** — scenarios, rubric, methodology, protocols, documentation —
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
You may share and adapt this material for any purpose, including commercially, provided you
give appropriate credit and indicate whether changes were made.

**Code** — validation scripts, evaluation harness, grader tooling — MIT, full text in
`LICENSE`.

## Citation

Until a paper exists, cite the repository and the version tag. See `CITATION.cff`.

## A request about naming

Modified copies of the scenario set should not be presented as official ViWELL releases. This
is a request about naming, not a restriction on use: CC BY 4.0 permits modification and
redistribution, and we are not narrowing it.
